# v0.3 — Connected (Week 3)

**What changes from v0.2:** the sources stop being files you maintain and start being your
live tools. The system reads what's true right now.

**Point it at your own work** — your team's board, your side project, whatever you'd check
first thing tomorrow. SnapLedger was the sandbox; v0.3 is yours.

---

## The scheduled task

**Cadence:** Weekdays, 08:00 (or whenever you'd rather already know)

```
Every weekday at 08:00:

1. Using the Atlassian connector, list every issue in [PROJECT-KEY] that changed status in
   the last 24 hours, and every issue currently blocked or flagged at risk.
2. Using [MEETING TOOL], read yesterday's meeting transcripts. Extract anything I committed
   to, and anything committed to me.
3. Cross-reference the two: name any commitment from step 2 that has no corresponding ticket
   from step 1.

Write a dated entry to standup-brief.md with three sections:

MOVED — what changed, and who moved it
STUCK — what's blocked, how long it's been blocked, who owns it
UNTICKETED — commitments with no ticket. These are the ones I'll get asked about.

Under 200 words. If a section is empty, say so in one line. Never invent a ticket reference;
if you can't find one, say "no ticket found".
```

---

## What good looks like

The UNTICKETED section is the one that earns its place. Everything above it you could have
found yourself in three tabs; that section catches the thing you forgot you promised.

Real example from a test run:

> **UNTICKETED** — In Tuesday's design sync you agreed to decide on the onboarding flow by
> Thursday. No ticket found.

---

## Variations worth trying

- **No Jira?** Swap step 1 for your own tracker, or a project folder, or a shared doc.
- **No meeting tool?** Run steps 1 and 3 only; add your calendar for "what's coming today".
- **Too noisy?** Add: "Only include items on the current sprint" or "ignore sub-tasks".
- **Too quiet?** Widen the window to 72 hours over a weekend.
