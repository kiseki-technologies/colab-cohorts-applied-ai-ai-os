# Meeting recorder — Granola or equivalent

The one integration that is entirely *your* data, needs no admin approval, and powers the
best line in the pre-standup brief: what you promised and never ticketed.

## Setup (Granola)

1. Install Granola and connect your calendar so it joins meetings automatically.
2. Claude Desktop → **Settings → Connectors** → add Granola → authorise.
3. Verify: `What did I commit to in my meetings this week?`

## If you use something else

Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, Zoom's own transcription, or your company's tool — check whether it
offers a Claude connector or an MCP server. Many do.

**If it has no connector:** export transcripts to a folder your Claude project can read. Less
elegant, works fine, and the prompts in this kit are unchanged apart from the source line.

**If you record nothing:** start. Even a month of transcripts changes what your system can do.
Which brings us to the rule below.

## Consent, always

Tell participants you're recording and transcribing. Every time, no exceptions. In some
jurisdictions it's a legal requirement; everywhere it's the difference between a tool your
colleagues trust and one they resent.

## What to ask it

```
What did I commit to this week, and to whom?
What decisions were taken in [MEETING], and what was the reasoning?
Which commitments from my meetings have no ticket in [PROJECT]?
```
