# v0.1 — The competitor watch (Week 1)

**What it proves:** AI can work while you sleep. Nothing here needs an integration —
just a schedule and a folder.

---

## The scheduled task

**Cadence:** Weekdays, 07:00

```
Every weekday morning, check [COMPETITOR A], [COMPETITOR B] and [COMPETITOR C] — their
websites, changelogs, blogs, and any news published in the last 24 hours.

Append a dated entry to competitor-digest.md in this folder. For each item report:
- what changed
- the source link
- one line on why it might matter to us

If nothing material has changed, write the date and "nothing material." Do not pad.
Never report an item without a source link.
```

---

## What good looks like

A file you can scroll back through. Each entry short enough to read standing up.
Quiet days genuinely say "nothing material" rather than dressing up a blog post as news.

## What to change first

If entries feel like noise, you don't need a better model — you need v0.2.
