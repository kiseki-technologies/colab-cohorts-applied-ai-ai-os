# Pre-meeting one-pager

Walk in knowing exactly where you left off, without spending twenty minutes remembering.

**Cadence:** triggered, or scheduled 30 minutes before recurring meetings
**Needs:** calendar + meetings + tracker

---

## The prompt

```
I have [MEETING] with [PERSON/GROUP] at [TIME].

Prepare a one-pager:
- what we last agreed, and when (from my meeting transcripts)
- what has moved since, in [PROJECT-KEY]
- anything I committed to them that isn't done
- anything they committed to me that isn't done
- the two or three things I actually need from this meeting
- one risk I should raise even though it would be easier not to

Under one page. If we've never met about this before, say so and give me context on
[TOPIC] instead.
```

---

## What good looks like

"Anything I committed to them that isn't done" is the line that saves your credibility.
Walking in already knowing you're late on something — and saying so first — is a completely
different meeting from being told.

The "risk I should raise even though it would be easier not to" line is deliberate. Use it.
