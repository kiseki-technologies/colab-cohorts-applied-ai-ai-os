# Competitor watch

The Week 1 original. No integrations needed — this one works from day one on any plan.

**Cadence:** weekdays, early
**Needs:** nothing but web access

See `1-standing-instructions/` for the full v0.1 → v0.2 → v0.3 progression of this recipe,
which is the clearest illustration in the kit of how a system matures:

- **v0.1** reports what changed
- **v0.2** assesses it against your research and strategy
- **v0.3** connects it to your live tools

If you only read one thing in this kit to understand *how* to improve an automation, read
those three files in order.
